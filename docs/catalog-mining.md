# Catalog mining method

Mining answers a limited guest-code question. Topic manifests keep the
evidence. Catalogs keep reusable symbols, layouts, vtables, slots and explicit
relations. Formats and tools: [topic manifests](topic-manifests.md),
[catalogs](catalogs.md), [headless workflow](workflow.md).

## Investigation scope

Define one falsifiable question, exact seeds and minimum reads. Expand at most
eight direct callers and callees, fewer where appropriate. Prefer one foreground
read-only query with related seeds. Identify exact functions before corroborating
with the recompiled copy. Instructions, decompilation, reports and logs stay in
ignored scratch.

Stop on image mismatch, lock, unresolved dispatch or writer, exceeded fan-out or
required runtime evidence. Negative results record premise, read set, result and
remaining alternatives.

## Recording results

1. Confirm `manifests/image.json` identity.
2. Record one finding, within a stated limit, in a topic manifest.
3. Promote reusable entities and explicit relations only. Proximity, shape and
   endpoint evidence do not prove an edge.
4. Allocate under [Catalog contract](catalogs.md), preserving IDs and reservations.
5. Record locators, confidence, qualifications, guards, and the alternative or
   falsifier.
6. Delete scratch after facts have a canonical home.

Static evidence supports static claims; behavior requires independently
reviewable runtime observation.
