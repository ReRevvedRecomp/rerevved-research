# Barbarian spawning and population limits

Evidence terms: findings cover the stated static branches, not observed spawn
cadence or delivered actions. Returns, live identity, ownership, retained state
and completed rebuild/order remain conditions wherever specified below.

## Raging behavior and implementer contract

Spawning can increase departure opportunities but does not reproduce Beta
aggression. `BAGRESSIVE` and `BFECUNDITY` are separate. Three guards have a
conditional city hold/release explanation, not a universal hut rule.

| Question | Verdict and native boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Three units trigger departure? | Conditional. `0x82CB3F08` takes a city ordinal, checks its owner against the barbarian ordinal and returns `R=3-a` for signed `a=BAGRESSIVE`, except `a=0` returns 99. At `0x82CC1308..0x82CC1324`, weighted defenders `C<=R` clear the current unit's selected target. `C>R` retains the selected-neighbor path only when unit byte `+4==0` and the other holds are bypassed. Default `a=1` therefore admits `C=3`; Beta `a=2` admits `C=2`. |
| Who moves and attacks? | Conditional. The current input unit can keep its selected neighbor; no instruction sends every city unit out. Eight-neighbor scoring, existing directions, destination and origin markers, definition flags and fallback selection still apply. Local codes/stores require later delivery and execution before they establish a move or attack. |
| Is this fecundity, era or year? | The barbarian threshold branch reads `BAGRESSIVE` at `Q+0`, with no fecundity, clock or era input on that local branch. General AI candidate selection separately has the turn/year and stat dependencies described below. |
| Is rebuilt presence fresh at production? | Conditional. The actual live child and result remain unidentified, and whether the completed scan supplies the later read remains unproved. Matching the accepted child class closes its accessor's return/effects, not the whole rebuild or invocation order. |

`C` is defender byte `0x830E6E90+40*x+y`: filtered configured-pool units add 1
for `+4==0`, otherwise 2, modulo 256. Production `W` instead uses 1/3. Types
3..5 have attack=defense=1 and pass definition masks. Three matching ordinary
records give `C=3` only with fresh rebuild, matching coordinates, no other
contribution/wrap. Nonzero `+4`, stale plane, invalid city or other records
invalidate simple headcount; live ownership/displayed hut remain inputs.

Hold also needs source halfword bit 0, valid `0x83128D08` city, `flags&0x87==0`
and defense nonzero. Marked destination bypasses; attack>defense has support
bypass, unlike equal-stat barbarian types. Origin 2/4 is separate. Cleared
target can enter fallback, not guaranteed stillness.

Spawning-only override belongs at eligible CityUpdate fecundity consumer,
preserving W/mode/production/cost/allocation/scheduling guards; no fixed
multiplier, forced attacks or Beta parity. Separate aggression control is
city-ordinal branch `0x82CB3F38..0x82CB3F74`: `a=2` -> `R=1`, default `a=1/R=2`.
Preserve owner check/state/return. Whole preset/global Q changes unrelated
settings; no aggression feature is implemented here.

Runtime acceptance must track same unit/city through `a/R`, C/W, weight,
neighbor, holds/fallback and executed command. Eight-function/748-word evidence:
`ragingBehaviorBoundary`.

from extra mode-zero calls `0x82D1FD20`/`0x82D1FDC8`, so these prove no spawn
interval. Local mechanism proves neither recurring successful spawns, fresh
guard count, population cap nor departure frequency. Formula/publication/
order/retention: `recurringProductionBoundary`.

## Fecundity and rebuilt presence

`BFECUNDITY` is Q+2: default 1, Beta 2, custom matched bits. Failure application
`(-1,0)` resets it; earlier preset does not identify later retained value. For
two aligned inputs, fill clears exactly 128 Q bytes or 1,600 presence bytes,
returning destination. Rebuild adds eligible configured-pool weights 1/3 modulo
256 at earlier sampled coordinates, not a fresh guard count.

Callback intervenes between coordinates/contribution. Retained root/frame at
`0x82D12AD4..0x82D12ADC` loads `M=[0x8314EFE0]`, `S=[M+0x10]`; null bypass
supplies -1 at `0x82D12B00`. Matching registered S/vptr `0x8215FA78` selects
`+0x100` -> two-instruction `0x82A73340`, returning `[S+0x20]` without
writes/other register changes. Next `+0xC` must be readable; allocation can
leave zero. Actual child/class/result, later dispatch, scan retention and
ordered same-cell `0x82D15408` read remain conditional.

Fecundity 1 admits presence 0/1, credits 1; 2 admits 0/1/2, credits 3. Zero mode
suppresses nonzero presence. Speed tests exactly 2, not a linear multiplier;
these formula cases prove neither cadence nor safe mod policy. Details:
`recurringProductionBoundary.inputBindingBoundary`.

## Recount Militia request

current city's signed owner/coordinates, not neighboring barbarian owner. City
owner must match session result `+0xC` and second local-player result; first

`<8` selects eight neighbors, not live-unit cap. Two source bytes must be zero;

guards consume earlier weighted bytes, not proved fresh counts.

No local random draw/threshold was identified; helper is no proved probability
gate. This neighboring-barbarian Militia request establishes neither recurring
barbarian spawning nor population limit.

## Date, turns and era

Current turn affects general AI candidate selection. One guarded turn/pair test
can install the current neighbor directly. A separate score adjustment applies
when the signed current turn has remainder zero modulo four; it has no barbarian
exemption. Barbarians bypass a different turn plus coordinate score rejection.

These are deterministic local decisions, with other guards and the origin-count
restriction still in force. They do not prove departure every four turns or
identify an actual hut invocation.

Displayed year has a separate use: under other-player and unnamed halfword
guards, year>=0 skips calls and state writes before common score handling. That
predicate does not directly select a direction. The skipped callees can affect
later inputs, so indirect scoring effects remain unresolved.

Current turn, configured start turn, displayed year, era and technology
ownership are distinct. Accepted Calendar evidence binds elapsed-turn and
era-dependent technology grants; those grants do not establish a spawn timer.
The effective attack/defense bodies have no direct clock or era read.

Their cumulative era-bonus lookup excludes the actual barbarian owner, and types
3..5 bypass attack's direct technology arm. Opaque selector10, a signed
configuration addend and inserted host stat helpers still prevent a complete
conclusion that scaling does not occur.

No confirmed link between retained scenario and game fields proves that Beta
Centauri's date accelerates spawning or hut departure. The exact branches,
four-function read set, 2,820 verified native words and required
return/state/action joins are in the manifest's `timePolicyBoundary`.

At `0x82CC055C..0x82CC05AC`, the selected adjustment adds `10*(A[I]-w)` to an
intermediate score, where `w` is 1 or 3 according to the unit's current `+4`
byte. Its inspected producer accumulates eligible records from configured unit
pools into A. It uses attack>=defense and other definition filters, adds the
same weights, and can wrap modulo 256.

It has no barbarian-only or hut filter on that contribution path.

The reader saves its origin index before earlier helper calls, then reloads unit
fields and A later. The producer also retains an early coordinate index while
reloading fields after calls. Interpreting the subtraction as other-unit support
needs a matching live unit, unchanged weight and coordinates, a completed
retained rebuild and no wrap. Those joins remain unproved.

Two located caller ingresses load a saved player scalar and an earlier
selected-unit sample. A helper runs between those loads, so actual values, the
reaching saved-word definition and retained register/frame state remain
qualified. Neither edge nor the source tile's unnamed bit identifies a barbarian
hut.

The next helper, `0x82CBC8E8`, has concrete native save/restore slots for caller
r27/r31. Its returning continuations restore those values if the frame and saved
bytes remain intact. It also explicitly writes unit coordinates and flags.

Keeping the same player/unit registers therefore does not keep the pre-call unit
contents or prove that the later AI origin matches an earlier A contribution.
Its output word and returned scalar are separate results.

Changing this score term could affect general AI candidates; it is not an
established control over hut release or spawn rate. The manifest's
`timePolicyBoundary.originInputRetentionBoundary.callerRetentionBoundary` owns
the caller, conditional return and memory limits.

## Rate and limit boundary

The routine responsible for city production and its local credit inputs are
identified above. Actual scheduling and the policy limiting the active barbarian
population remain open. The 128 city records are a storage walk, and the common
creator's 256 per-player unit slots are storage capacity. Neither establishes a
Raging Barbarians population policy.

The other inspected creation arms included copy-like and paired-unit operations,
a Spy grant, a nonbarbarian city-fill arm and a Calendar grant that skipped the
barbarian player. In particular, the city-fill arm's returned-index comparison
with 128 is not a barbarian population maximum. Movement, attack policy and
scenario-specific behavior remain separate.
